01 / THE LIBRARYMADE FOR SOUND

01 / FEATURES

Stay in the flow.

Less time looking through folders. More time finding the right sound.

01

Find your sound

Search names, paths, categories, tags, and notes. Narrow results with filters like favorites, rating, duration, and BPM.

02

Listen as you browse

Preview audio, see its waveform, and use the space bar to play or pause.

03

Make it yours

Edit display names, categories, tags, ratings, and notes. Group sounds in collections without moving the originals.

04

Drag into your work

Take the original audio file from your library into Logic Pro, without creating an intermediate audio copy.

05

Keep it local

Work with folders and external drives. Your audio and library metadata stay on your Mac.

06

Review suggestions

Local analysis uses audio measurements and path information to suggest metadata. You decide what to accept, edit, or reject.
